# Student Experience

## Student portal

Place the shortcode below on the WordPress page students use after login:

`[student_drive_portal]`

## What students can receive

Depending on teacher configuration, students can receive:

- Courses in which they are enrolled.
- Directly assigned Drive content.
- Released lessons.
- Approved course resources connected to Google Drive.
- Scheduled lessons after their release time.

## Recommended student dashboard design

The V5 roadmap is moving toward a complete student dashboard with:

- My Courses
- Continue Learning
- Progress bars
- Upcoming deadlines
- Announcements
- Grades
- Certificates
- Bookmarks
- Recent activity
- Ask My Course AI

## Access rules

Students should never browse the teacher's full Google Drive. Assignment should be limited to enrolled courses, released lessons, direct assignments, and approved Teaching Libraries.

## Completion data

Current progress calculations use enrolled courses, course lessons, and completed-lesson metadata. The completion engine will become more comprehensive as V5 assessment and prerequisite systems are implemented.
